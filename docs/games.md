# Game Types Reference

| Game | Min Players | Max Players | Rounds | Decision Guidance |
|------|:-----------:|:-----------:|:------:|-------------------|
| **Prisoner's Dilemma** | 2 | 2 | 4 | Both players choose at once. Mutual cooperation pays well, but defecting against a cooperator is the jackpot. If both defect, everyone suffers. Trust — or exploit? |
| **Stag Hunt** | 4 | 6 | 3 | Hunt stag for a big payoff — but only if enough players join you. Rabbit is safe but modest. The question: can you count on the group to coordinate? |
| **Golden Ball** | 4 | 6 | 3 | Split shares the pool equally. Steal takes everything — unless the other player also steals, then nobody gets a thing. One shot, no take-backs. |
| **Beauty Contest** | 4 | 6 | 3 | Pick a number 0–100. The winner is closest to ⅔ of the group's average. Think about what others will guess — then think one level deeper. |
| **Ultimatum Game** | 2 | 2 | 1 | The proposer divides 100 points; the responder accepts or rejects. Rejection means both get zero. How much is "fair enough" to avoid spite? |
| **Public Goods Game** | 4 | 6 | 3 | Contribute to the shared pool — it gets multiplied 1.6× and split equally. Contributing nothing lets you free-ride. But if everyone free-rides, the pool stays empty. |
| **Volunteer's Dilemma** | 3 | 6 | 3 | If at least one player volunteers, everyone benefits — but volunteering has a cost. If nobody steps up, everyone gets nothing. Will you take one for the team? |
| **Colonel Blotto** | 3 | 6 | 3 | Distribute exactly 6 troops across 4 battlefields. Whoever deploys more troops wins that field. Win the most battlefields to win the round. |
| **All-Pay Auction** | 4 | 6 | 3 | Bid for a 100-point prize. Highest bidder wins — but every player pays their bid, win or lose. Overbid and you burn points; underbid and you lose your stake. |
| **Trolley Problem** | 4 | 6 | 1 | Two moral dilemmas: the classic lever and the fat-man variant. No payoff matrix — just your ethical instinct. Your answers reveal your moral framework to the group. |
